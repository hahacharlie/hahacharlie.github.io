Research

Agentic AI for chip design, and the hardware underneath it

My research interests are electronic design automation, computer architecture, and heterogeneous integration IC design. Recent work builds LLM and multi-agent workflows for physical design parameter optimization, FPGA place-and-route algorithm evolution, and cross-layer optimization of 2.5D/3D chiplet-based systems; earlier work covers hardware priority queue architectures on FPGA and SRAM design in ASAP7.
