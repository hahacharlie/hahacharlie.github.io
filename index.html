Qihang (Charlie) Wu

I build AI agents that help design better chips.

I am a Ph.D. student advised by Prof. Vidya A. Chhabria, and I collaborate with Prof. Aman Arora on computer architecture. My research develops LLM-based agents for electronic design automation, with a focus on physical design, FPGA algorithms, and 2.5D/3D chiplet systems.

Previously, I was a hardware systems engineer at Artly AI. I earned my M.S. in Electrical Engineering from NYU and dual B.S. degrees in Computer Science and Mechanical Engineering from the University of Portland.
