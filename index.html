Qihang (Charlie) Wu

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Arizona State University, advised by Prof. Vidya A. Chhabria, and I work closely with Prof. Aman Arora on computer architecture. My research is in electronic design automation: I build LLM-guided agents that optimize EDA algorithms and explore architectures for heterogeneous (2.5D/3D) integration, with broader interests in computer architecture and SRAM and circuit design. I hold an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from NYU Tandon School of Engineering and dual B.S. degrees in Computer Science and Mechanical Engineering from the University of Portland. Before graduate school, I was a hardware system engineer at Artly AI, a robotics startup in Seattle.
