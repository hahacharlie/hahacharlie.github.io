Qihang (Charlie) Wu

Qihang (Charlie) Wu is an M.S. student in Electrical Engineering at Arizona State University (since January 2026), and holds an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from NYU Tandon School of Engineering and a dual B.S. in Computer Science and Mechanical Engineering from the University of Portland. Charlie's research spans LLM agents for electronic design automation, heterogeneous integration, computer architecture, and SRAM and circuit design, with prior industry experience as a hardware system engineer at Artly AI.
